# Celebrating 500 Days Seizure-Free: A Father's Gift to the Epilepsy Community

This repository is being released to celebrate a precious milestone: **500 days seizure-free** for my son, Mario. 

## Our Journey

As a father of a child with epilepsy, I've experienced firsthand the challenges, fears, and hopes that come with this condition. Every seizure-free day is a victory, and reaching 500 days is a milestone that once seemed impossible.

MirrorHR was born from necessity - the need to better understand, monitor, and manage epilepsy. What started as a tool for our family has grown into something that can potentially help thousands of others facing similar challenges.

## Why We're Sharing This

By releasing this work as open source, I hope to:

1. **Give back to the community** that supported us during difficult times
2. **Empower researchers** with real-world data to advance epilepsy research
3. **Help other families** find better ways to monitor and understand seizure patterns
4. **Inspire developers** to build tools that make life better for people with neurological conditions

## For Mario and Every Child Like Him

This release is dedicated to Mario and every child living with epilepsy worldwide. Your resilience inspires us every day.

If this project helps even one family gain better insights into their loved one's condition or contributes to research that reduces seizures for others, then it will have fulfilled its purpose.

## Join Us

I invite researchers, developers, healthcare professionals, and families affected by epilepsy to use this tool, contribute to its development, and help us create a world where more children can celebrate their own seizure-free milestones.
