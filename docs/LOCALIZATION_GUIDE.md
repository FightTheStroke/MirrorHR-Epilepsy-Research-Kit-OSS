# Localization Guide

## Overview

This guide provides comprehensive information for localizing MirrorHR. The application supports multiple languages and follows Apple's localization best practices.

We believe it is fundamental to co-design and co-develop localization with patients, for patients. The language and cultural needs of those living with epilepsy and their families are central to our approach, ensuring that all translations and localizations are meaningful and accessible.
