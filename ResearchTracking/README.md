# Research Publication Tracking

This directory contains the MirrorHR research publication tracking system, which helps us monitor and showcase how our data is being used in scientific research.

## Overview

When researchers use MirrorHR data in their studies, we ask them to register their publications. This allows us to:

1. Build a community of epilepsy researchers
2. Showcase the impact of MirrorHR in advancing epilepsy research
3. Connect researchers working on similar topics
4. Demonstrate the value of our open data approach

## How It Works

1. Researchers submit their publication details via:
   - Direct email to [info@fightthestroke.org](mailto:info@fightthestroke.org)
   - Pull requests to this repository

2. Submissions are reviewed by the FightTheStroke team
3. Approved publications are added to `publications.json` and showcased on our website

## Publications JSON Format

The `publications.json` file follows a standardized format for tracking research publications. See the file for detailed schema information.

## Submission Requirements

All submissions must include:
- Publication title
- Author list
- Journal/conference name
- Publication date
- DOI (if available)
- Abstract
- How MirrorHR data was used in the research
- Contact email for the corresponding author

## For Researchers

If you're using MirrorHR data in your research:

1. Review our [usage guidelines](../RealData/README.md#usage-guidelines)
2. Properly cite MirrorHR as described in our [citation format](../RealData/README.md#citation-format)
3. Submit your publication details when your work is published
4. Consider reaching out to [info@fightthestroke.org](mailto:info@fightthestroke.org) for collaboration opportunities

Thank you for contributing to epilepsy research and helping build our community of researchers working to reduce the number and severity of epileptic seizures.
